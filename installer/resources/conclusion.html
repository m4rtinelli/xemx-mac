XEMX is installed

Ableton Live: open Settings → Plug-Ins, turn on Audio Units and/or VST3 System Folders, then click Rescan. XEMX appears under Plug-Ins.

Logic Pro: XEMX appears under Instruments → AU Instruments → XEMX.

Drum samples: put your EMX-1 sample folder or ZIP in Downloads/ELECTRIBE, or pick it with the SAMPLES button on the panel.

Patterns: drag a .emx file onto the plug-in or click LOAD .EMX.
