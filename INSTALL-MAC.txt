XEMX for macOS (universal: Apple Silicon + Intel)

1. Copy the plugins:
     XEMX.vst3       -> ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
     XEMX.component  -> ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
     XEMX.app        -> /Applications (optional standalone)

2. The build is ad-hoc signed, not notarized. Clear the quarantine flag once in Terminal:
     xattr -cr ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/XEMX.vst3
     xattr -cr ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/XEMX.component
     xattr -cr /Applications/XEMX.app

3. In Ableton Live: Settings > Plug-Ins > enable "Use Audio Units" / "VST3 System Folders", then Rescan.

Samples: put your EMX-1 sample folder or ZIP in ~/Downloads/ELECTRIBE, or pick it with the SAMPLES button.
